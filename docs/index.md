# WhatsApp Bridge

![WhatsApp Bridge logo](assets/logo.png){ width="320" }

Send and receive WhatsApp messages from Home Assistant automations.

!!! warning "Unofficial WhatsApp client"
    This integration drives [`whatsapp-web.js`](https://wwebjs.dev/) inside a companion add-on.
    WhatsApp's Terms of Service do not authorize bot-style use of personal accounts, and your
    account may be banned, temporarily or permanently. **Use a secondary phone number, not your
    primary one.** You accept this risk by installing the integration.

## What you get

- A `notify.whatsapp` notify platform.
- Services: `send_text`, `send_media`, `send_location`, `contact_add`, `contact_update`,
  `contact_delete`, `session_logout`, `session_restart`.
- A `whatsapp_message_received` event for incoming messages.
- Sensors for connection state, phone battery and last message time, plus a connectivity
  binary sensor.
- An `image` entity that shows the pairing QR code.
- A sidebar panel (**WhatsApp**) with built-in QR pairing and a phonebook.
- An optional Lovelace card, `custom:whatsapp-qr-card`.

## Requirements

- Home Assistant OS or Supervised.
- The companion [WhatsApp Bridge add-on](https://github.com/bareli/whatsapp-bridge-addon)
  running on the same host.
- One spare WhatsApp account and phone number.
- Home Assistant 2024.10.0 or newer (from `hacs.json`).
- Architectures: amd64 and aarch64. A Raspberry Pi 3 (armv7) is not supported.
